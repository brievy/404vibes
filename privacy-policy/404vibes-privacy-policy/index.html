404Vibes Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

404Vibes Etsy Pinterest Studio is a private desktop application used to create Pinterest promotional content for the owner's Etsy shop.

Information Accessed

The application may access Etsy product listing information including product titles, descriptions, listing URLs, and product images.

When connected to Pinterest, the application may access the account's existing Pinterest boards and publish Pins authorized by the account owner.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to create and publish promotional Pinterest content for the owner's Etsy products.

Data Storage

API credentials and application settings are stored locally on the user's computer. 404Vibes does not operate a public database containing Etsy or Pinterest user information.

Third-Party Services

The application may communicate with Etsy, Pinterest, and OpenAI APIs when required to provide its functionality.

Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold to third parties.

User Control

The account owner controls which Pinterest board is used and approves content before publication.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact 404Vibes through the official 404Vibes Etsy shop.
