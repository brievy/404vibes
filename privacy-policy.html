404Vibes Etsy Pinterest Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

404Vibes Etsy Pinterest Studio is a private desktop application developed to help manage promotional content for the 404Vibes Etsy shop.

Information We Access

The application may access Etsy listing information such as product titles, descriptions, listing URLs, and product images.

When connected to Pinterest, the application may access Pinterest account information required to display existing boards and publish Pins authorized by the account owner.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to create and publish Pinterest promotional content for the account owner's Etsy products.

Data Storage

API credentials and application settings are stored locally on the user's computer. This application does not operate a public database for storing Etsy or Pinterest user information.

Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes. Information may be transmitted only to services required for the application's functionality, such as Etsy, Pinterest, and OpenAI APIs.

User Control

The account owner controls which Pinterest board is selected and must approve content before it is published.

Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the owner of 404Vibes through the official 404Vibes Etsy shop.
